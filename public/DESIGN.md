## Brand & Style

This design system draws its foundation from a modern, boutique indoor adventure park and family community hub. The aesthetic is warm, vibrant, and tactile—blending the joyous, boundless energy of childhood play with a curated, Scandinavian-leaning organic warmth that parents appreciate. 

The emotional signature is welcoming, safe, imaginative, and spirited ("Feim Tribu"). Instead of gaudy primary tones, the system leverages a Mediterranean playground palette: refreshing turquoise waters, sun-baked terracotta, golden honey mustard, and soft eucalyptus green, all resting on a soft vanilla cream foundation.

The design movement combines **Modern Tactile Minimalism** with **Organic Playfulness**:
- Generous, rounded pill contours evoking ball pits, sensory foam pads, and curved wooden slides.
- Asymmetric, buoyant sticker badges and celebratory confetti accents.
- Earthy, warm typography that balances personality-rich grotesque display headlines with exceptionally clean, rounded body text.
- Micro-interactions characterized by bouncy spring physics, subtle squishy tactile responses, and celebratory micro-moments.

## Colors

The color palette centers on cheerful warmth balanced by soothing natural tones. 

- **Primary (`#1CAFB4`)**: A vibrant turquoise lagoon tone. Signifies exploration, slides, ball pit splash areas, and active play. Acts as the primary anchor for CTAs, active states, and brand highlights.
- **Secondary (`#F29774`)**: A playful terracotta-coral. Used for celebratory moments, birthday party indicators, high-energy accents, and energetic badges.
- **Tertiary (`#F5A253`)**: A warm golden honey/apricot. Ideal for secondary alerts, warm badges, seasonal announcements, and creative workshop highlights.
- **Neutral (`#594037`)**: Deep toasted cocoa brown. Replaces harsh blacks to maintain a warm, human, and grounded organic tone across high-contrast typography, outlines, and structural frames.
- **Canvas Cream (`#FDF8ED`)**: The primary surface background across the experience, ensuring soft glare-free viewing and an inviting, natural wood-and-linen ambiance.
- **Sage Eucalyptus (`#84A98C`)**: Evokes the outdoors, teepee campouts, and natural wooden play elements. Perfect for health, safety, and calming informational callouts.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs the spirited personality of **Bricolage Grotesque** with the legibility and friendly geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Headlines & Feature Badges**: `Bricolage Grotesque` delivers optical quirks, cheerful rounded stroke ends, and exuberant charm. It mimics wooden blocks, signage, and energetic shouts of delight without losing design discipline.
- **Body, UI Controls, & Operational Text**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides pristine modern clarity, generous x-height, and soft geometric terminals. It keeps reservation schedules, admission fees, opening hours, and safety instructions effortlessly digestible for busy parents on mobile devices.
- **Rhythm**: Always maintain generous line height across body text to preserve a relaxing, breathable reading experience.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system paired with organic spatial sections.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px edge margins. Stacked cards, sticky bottom reservation bars.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px edge margins.
  - **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12 columns, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, max container width capped at `1200px` (`75rem`).

- **Playful Asymmetry & Wavy Transitions**:
  - Content blocks frequently employ soft alternating offsets (e.g., photo galleries arranged like playful photo stickers on cork boards).
  - Major section transitions (such as transitioning from Hero to Attractions, or Pricing to Parties) feature gentle wave/curved dividers instead of rigid horizontal rules.

## Elevation & Depth

To reflect physical playground structures (cushioned mats, wooden frames, smooth plastic tunnels), elevation uses **soft, warm ambient cushioning** instead of sharp, cold digital drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Surface background in warm cream (`#FDF8ED`). Borders are either absent or defined by subtle 1.5px warm clay outlines (`rgba(89, 64, 55, 0.08)`).
- **Level 1 (Cushioned Cards)**: Subtle ambient lift tinted with warm brown: `0 4px 14px -2px rgba(89, 64, 55, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(89, 64, 55, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Elements & Floating Badges)**: Bouncy depth for buttons and interactive chips: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 175, 180, 0.22)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sticky Booking Bars)**: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(89, 64, 55, 0.16)`.
- **Tactile Squish Effect**: Interactive cards and buttons visually translate down by 2px on `:active` with an inner or tighter shadow, simulating a cushioned bounce.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy mirrors child safety: zero sharp edges. Everything is soft, pill-shaped, or rounded with generous border radiuses:

- **Pill Containers (`roundedness: 3`)**: Buttons, badges, status pills, and interactive filter chips utilize full pill contours (`9999px` or `2rem+`).
- **Cards & Enclosures**: Cards use `24px` to `32px` corner radii (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`), resembling rounded wooden play furniture and soft play blocks.
- **Teepee & Tent Motifs**: Triangle indicators (such as tooltips, decorative accents, and section badges) feature rounded vertices to preserve the soft theme.
- **Wavy Edges**: Decorative hero separators and photo masks apply smooth organic scallops.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Pill-shaped, background in vibrant turquoise (`#1CAFB4`), text in pure white with bold `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Elevated with a subtle turquoise glow shadow. On hover, expands smoothly with a slight upward float (`transform: translateY(-2px)`). On active, presses down (`transform: translateY(1px)`).
- **Secondary Button**: Pill-shaped, warm terracotta (`#F29774`) background, text in white. Used for birthday bookings and celebration highlights.
- **Tertiary / Outline Button**: Cream surface with a 2px solid border in Toasted Cocoa (`#594037`), label in cocoa brown.

### Badges & Fun Stickers
- Pill-shaped or organic tilted stamps (-3deg to 3deg rotation).
- Micro-labels displaying key park features ("A partir de 1 any", "Zona Cafeteria", "Piscina de boles", "Climatitzat").
- Vivid color combinations: Sunny gold badge with deep cocoa text, or soft sage badge with deep forest text.

### Cards (Attractions & Party Packs)
- Background: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) layered atop canvas cream (`#FDF8ED`).
- Radius: `24px` with smooth interior padding (`24px`).
- Features: Top imagery framed by rounded pill masks, accompanied by small floating icon badges (e.g. 🎂 Birthday, ⛺ Teepee camp, 🎈 Ball pit).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Pill or heavily rounded (`16px`) input fields with warm cream/white background.
- Border: 1.5px subtle cocoa tint, transitioning to 2px turquoise (`#1CAFB4`) on focus with a soft focus-ring halo.
- Floating placeholder labels styled in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom rounded squares for checkboxes (`8px` radius) and circles for radio buttons.
- Checked state fills with turquoise (`#1CAFB4`) or terracotta (`#F29774`) accompanied by a crisp white checkmark.

### Specialized Tribe Playground Components
- **Party Tier Card**: Highlighting customizable birthday party packages with color-coded theme ribbons (Turquoise, Coral, Gold), price pill indicators, and breakdown check lists.
- **Capacity / Live Traffic Meter**: Pill progress bar showcasing real-time park capacity with gentle rounded segments.
- **Tribu Icon Badges**: Decorative circular badges with line-art icons representing cactus, teepees, party hats, slides, and sensory play.
